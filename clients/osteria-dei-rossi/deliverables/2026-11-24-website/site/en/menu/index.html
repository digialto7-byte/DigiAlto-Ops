Questions about the menu and the dishes

What are the house dishes?

The dishes marked Consigliato dall'Oste (recommended by the host) include the beef tartare, pici with traditional duck ragù, pici with white Cinta Senese ragù, the slow-cooked Cinta Senese stew and the bistecca alla fiorentina. Spaghetti alla chitarra with the osteria's own sauce has also been on the menu since 1992.

What is cinghiale in dolceforte?

It is a wild boar dish with dolceforte, a sweet-and-sour sauce that goes back to medieval cooking. The chocolate in today's recipe was added in later centuries. The dish is on the menu as Cinghiale in Dolce Forte.

What are pici, and how do you serve them?

Pici are thick, hand-rolled strands of pasta made from flour and water, typical of the Siena area. At the osteria they are served with white Cinta Senese ragù, duck ragù, cacio e pepe and wild boar ragù.

What is Cinta Senese?

Cinta Senese is the native pig breed of the Siena hills, dark with a white band, the ‘cinta’, around its shoulders. It is painted in Ambrogio Lorenzetti's fresco of 1338–39 in Siena's Palazzo Pubblico and has protected-origin (DOP) status. On the menu it appears in the pici with white ragù and in the tegamata.

What are cantucci and Vin Santo?

Cantucci are Tuscan almond biscuits; the tradition is to dip them in Vin Santo, the sweet Tuscan dessert wine, to end the meal. The osteria serves cantucci and Vin Santo, listed among the desserts.

How much is the cover charge?

The cover charge is 2.50 € per person. Steaks are sold by weight, at the price per 100 g shown on the menu. The weight is agreed at the table with the waiter.

How do you handle allergens and intolerances?

Please ask the staff before ordering: the allergen list for each dish is being confirmed and is not yet published on this site. It will not be published before the chef confirms it.
