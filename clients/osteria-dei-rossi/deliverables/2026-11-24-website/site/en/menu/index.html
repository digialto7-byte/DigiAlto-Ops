I Starters

  • Tagliere del Cacciatore

    22 €

    A selection of cured Cinta Senese pork and game meats, bringing the most authentic flavours of the local area to your table.

  • Tartare dell’Oste

    15 €

    The host recommends

    Beef tartare, hand-cut with a knife and prepared to order to bring out the quality of the meat and its authentic flavour. Served on a crispy Parmesan wafer and accompanied by antique wholegrain mustard.

  • Tagliere del Contadino

    16 €

    Tuscan pecorino cheeses paired with honey and preserves that bring all the goodness of the region straight to your table.

  • Bis di crostoni

    12 €

    Two toasted bread slices with very different flavours: one topped with Gorgonzola and slices of truffle, the other with Tuscan cheese, pears and honey. Two combinations that bring together bold flavours, creaminess and delicate sweet notes.

  • Acciughe sotto pesto

    10 €

    Anchovies with Senese tarragon pesto: fragrant, full of flavour and with that unexpected little something extra.

  • Crostini neri senesi

    9 €

    A true symbol of Senese cuisine, with an unmistakable classic flavour that brings the tastes of Siena straight to your table.

Steaks and grilled cuts

    12 € per 100 g
