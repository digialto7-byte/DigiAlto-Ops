# Competitor site teardown: osterie and restaurants in Siena's historic centre (on-page SEO and content, as of 24 November 2026)

**Read this first: evidence level of this file.** No competitor page could be opened directly in this session (details in section 1). Every field below therefore carries one of these labels:

- **OBSERVED** = read directly on the live page. *There are no OBSERVED fields in this file.*
- **SNIPPET** = taken from search results restricted to the competitor's own domain: the page title and URL as indexed, plus the search tool's summary of the indexed page text. The summary is a paraphrase by the search tool, not verbatim copy, and may reflect an older crawl. Where a SNIPPET comes from a third-party listing instead, the line says so.
- **INFERRED** = my deduction from SNIPPET evidence; the evidence is stated next to it.
- **not observable** = could not be seen with the tools available in this session. Not a statement that the thing is absent.

Meta descriptions, meta keywords, hreflang, JSON-LD, H1/H2, word counts, design, motion and speed are **not observable for every competitor** in this run. They are stated once per block as a single line to avoid repetition.

### Inferences
- WordPress is the most common platform in the set: Taverna di San Giuseppe, Da Divo, La Sosta di Violante, Papei, Fonte Giusta, Il Vinaio, Babazuf and probably Boccon del Prete. Le Logge is on Joomla, Nonna Gina and Vinattieri are hand-built, Grattacielo is on Google Sites. All of this is INFERRED from URL and title patterns, not from generator tags.
- Template and migration leftovers are widespread (Papei, La Sosta di Violante, Taverna di San Giuseppe, Gallo Nero, Il Vinaio), which signals low ongoing maintenance and gives a well-kept site an easy quality edge.
- The most common story device is the building, not the people: Etruscan cellars or rooms appear at Taverna di San Giuseppe, Da Divo, Gallo Nero and, via Michelin, Le Logge. Named individuals appear only at Permalico (owner), Nonna Gina (grandmother) and Grattacielo (business name). No summary mentioned a contrada.
- Every competitor that states a location uses Piazza del Campo as the anchor (300 metres, 100 metres, a few steps, two minutes).
