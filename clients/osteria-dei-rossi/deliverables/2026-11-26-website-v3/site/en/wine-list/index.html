The wine list of our osteria in Siena

From the brick grotto you go on to the cellar, where the wines of this list are kept. Three are the great reds of the Siena area: Chianti Classico with its Gallo Nero, the black rooster; Vino Nobile di Montepulciano; Brunello di Montalcino. Then Bolgheri, other Tuscan reds, whites and sparkling wines. Not sure what to choose? Ask us: we are glad to help.

Questions about the wines and the cellar

What wines are on the wine list?

The wine list includes the three great reds of the Siena area: Chianti Classico (the Gallo Nero), Vino Nobile di Montepulciano and Brunello di Montalcino. It also has Bolgheri, other reds, whites, rosé and sparkling wines. By the glass there are reds and whites, and by the bottle there is also Vino l'Osteria. Prices are not published online.

Which wines of the Siena area are on the list?

The three great reds of the Siena area are all on the list. There is Chianti Classico from Selvolini (Gallo Nero) and from Lamole di Lamole, plus Chianti Colli Senesi La Foresta. For Montepulciano there are Rosso and Nobile from Guidotti and from Poggio alla Sala; for Montalcino, Rosso and Brunello from Caparzo and from Camigliano.
