# Copy audit of version 2 and directions for version 3 (osteria-dei-rossi)

Author: copywriter. Date: 2026-11-26. Audit, not final copy: proposals are directions (IT, with EN mirror where useful).
Read: founder feedback 2026-11-26, profile.md, banned-claims.md, copy.md v2, built `index.html` and `en/index.html` (built text matches copy.md), live menu 2026-11-24, photos-2026-11-24.md.
Rule applied: no price, no dish row on the home page; dish named inside the explanation when it is worth it. Not reused: "le migliori carni", "esclusiva", "gli unici a Siena".

## 3. Long, vague or weak copy

- `casa` body_1 "Sedetevi, guardatevi intorno, ordinate quello che vi va. Il resto viene da sé." Generic; any osteria. Replace with one concrete welcome fact (the friendly service, Luca at the counter side, the room).
- `tavola` body "Cominciamo dal cinghiale, in due modi, e da un crostino nero." Signposting, not content. Cut to one sentence that states what the section gives: the stews, the terracotta, the medieval root.
- `dispensa` intro "Cosa sono, che storia hanno e come li portiamo in tavola" Pure meta. Delete.
- `cinta_body` 78 words, four ideas. Split: what it is (nativa, fascia bianca, Lorenzetti 1338-39, DOP) in two sentences; what you eat (Tegamata, Cacciatore, ragù) in one.
- `pici` body_2 "Scegliete quello che vi chiama, o assaggiateli tutti, una visita alla volta." Charming but empty. Keep the idea of coming back, shorten.
- `spaghetti_body` "Un incontro deciso di sapori, rosso e piccante." Echo of menu. The sentence that works: "Se è la vostra prima volta da noi, potete cominciare da qui." Keep it, move up.
- `contrada` palio_line "Il Palio si corre il 2 luglio e il 16 agosto." Stands alone, no link to the osteria. Either tie it to an approved fact (menu: Trippa alla Senese "particolarmente legata al periodo del Palio") or move to SEO/FAQ.
- `cantina` body is good; `amari_line` is a product list. One clause is enough.
- Target for v3: each block = one sensory line + one fact + one named dish. Aim at 35 to 50 words per block (v2 average is about 55 to 75). Every sentence either names a dish, a place or a fact; no "dice il menù", no meta lines. Keep the best sensory lines as they are: "Quando il piatto arriva, il profumo vi ha già avvisato." and "Tre modi, un solo fungo".

**Booking path**
- "Prenota" is a bare verb. Nav and hero buttons scroll to `#prenota`, while the mobile bar says only "Chiama" and "WhatsApp". Direction: label the bar "Prenota: chiama" / "Prenota su WhatsApp" (EN "Book: call" / "Book on WhatsApp").
- The hero has no phone number and no hours, only "Da martedì a sabato; domenica solo a pranzo". Direction: add "pranzo e cena" and the number as text; the full hours stay in `prenota`.
- Keep "Gli orari possono cambiare: chiamateci per conferma" next to every hours mention. No reply-time promise (not an approved fact). A pre-filled WhatsApp message is a copy slot to decide with the founder.
- Menu links: use two wordings only. "Guarda il menù" (hero) and one-line section links such as "Nel menù: prezzi e tutti i secondi" (see 6). Do not write "prezzi" as a teaser in more than one place.

## 5. Marketing hooks from approved facts (under-used)

- **1992 and the opening by Beppe Carrieri**: used six times as a date, never as a story. Put it once, strongly (contrada), and cut the five echoes. Do not write "più di trent'anni" without the reviewer's say (arithmetic from the approved year).
- **Gambero Rosso**: a small grey note under the fiorentina. Profile says "listed among Siena's restaurants for meat". V2 says "scelti da" / "chose": stronger than the source. Direction: use "segnalata da Gambero Rosso tra i ristoranti di Siena per la carne" once, near the hero or fiorentina headline, in the profile's own verb.
- **Contrada del Bruco and Porta Ovile**: gate animation and one paragraph. Could work in the hero kicker ("nella Contrada del Bruco").
- **Palio, 2 July and 16 August**: a bare line. Pair with the menu's own Trippa alla Senese line ("legata al periodo del Palio"). Do not say the osteria is open on those days.
- **The room**: the strongest asset (beams, brick vaults, terracotta, lanterns, counter on the kitchen, photographs of Siena). Founder wants it bigger: caption each frame with one concrete thing.
- **Cinta Senese**: Lorenzetti 1338-39 and DOP are the best "fact" hook on the page. Move earlier in the block.
- **"Consigliato dall'Oste"**: approved flag, lost with the rows. Say it in prose ("il piatto che l'Oste consiglia") for the anatra, Tegamata and fiorentina.
- **5 minutes on foot from Piazza del Campo**, **UNESCO since 1995** (centre of Siena): unused on the home page. The first one fits the hero lead or the booking block; both are strong for local search.
- **Medieval dolceforte with later chocolate**: good, keep, and make it the headline of that block.

## 7. Risks

**Sentences that lean on something the profile does not support**
1. `cinta` caption "Il gran tagliere di salumi" sits in the Cinta block. Photo 8 shows prosciutto, salame, lardo, crostini: nothing says it is Cinta. Move the photo to the taglieri block.
2. Truffle "fresco, tutto l'anno" (founder caption) next to menu "Tagliatelle al Tartufo di Stagione" and the porcini "di stagione" in the adjacent block. The deck admits it does not reconcile them. Ask the founder; until then avoid "di stagione" and "tutto l'anno" in the same block.
3. `casa` "Dal bancone si vede la cucina: lì c'è Luca". Source says Luca is in the kitchen, not that he is visible at the counter at every service. Direction: "in cucina c'è Luca".
4. `contrada` "ispirazione, per chi cucina e per chi siede a tavola". Source says only "as inspiration". "Per chi cucina" is added.
5. `contrada` "la nostra porta è lungo la discesa". Inferred from the street shape; the profile does not locate no. 79/81.
6. `fiorentina` "Siamo tra gli indirizzi scelti da Gambero Rosso" (IT) and "chose" (EN): source says "listed ... for meat". See 5.
7. `spaghetti` "da quando l'osteria ha aperto". The menu says "in menù dal 1992"; the profile says the osteria opened in 1992. The link is plausible, not stated.
8. `porcini` "Li trovate ... alla griglia ... fritti": those two dishes are not on the live menu; the phrase implies availability. Add no promise.
9. `dolce` "Da noi arrivano su un piatto, con un bicchierino di Vin Santo": from one photo; keep but avoid "sempre".
10. `tavola` "Da noi arriva a tavola nel coccio": photo-based for the stews only; the menu does not say every stew is served in it.
- Checked and fine: the two-handled bowl and bay leaf for the dolceforte (photo 17 is captioned dolce forte); Lorenzetti and DOP; Luca and Max since 2024; hours and call-to-confirm.

**IT and EN lines that differ in strength or sense**
- No outright contradictions found in the built pages.
- "con la ciccia buona" (IT, colloquial) is "good meat" in EN: tone softened; quote is verbatim from the menu so keep the IT line shown under it, as v2 does.
- Gambero Rosso: IT "scelti", EN "chose": both stronger than the source.
- "coccio", "castellare", "brucaiolo", "contrada", "Tegamata": left unglossed in EN (`in the coccio` is explained only in the body; `brucaiolo` never). Gloss once in EN or avoid in headings.
- "Cantucci" (heading, profile) and "Cantuccini" (menu name): both languages mix them. Choose "cantucci" in text and keep "Cantuccini" only as the menu dish name.
- EN title and meta use "Sienese food" where IT says "cucina senese": fine, but align the dish words (pici, wild boar in dolceforte, Cinta Senese) in both.
