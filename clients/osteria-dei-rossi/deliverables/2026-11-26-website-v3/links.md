# Osteria dei Rossi website, version 3: links for the owner

Internal hand-over note, English. Every address below is fixed: it will not change with later versions,
so it is safe on printed QR codes, in the Google Business Profile and in social posts.
`www.osteriadeirossi.it` is the domain the founder chose; the site is not online yet.

## Where each place sends people

| Use | Italian link | English link | What the visitor sees first |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile, "Website" | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/` | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/en/` | The gate opening, then the first screen with address, phone and "Prenota un tavolo" |
| Google Business Profile, "Menu" | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/menu/` | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/en/menu/` | The menu with prices (the only page with prices) |
| Google Business Profile, "Reservations" | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/#prenota` | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/en/#prenota` | Call and WhatsApp buttons with the opening hours |
| Instagram and Facebook profile link | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/#porta` | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/en/#porta` | The first screen at once, without the opening animation |
| Hotels, guides, Maps | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/dove-siamo/` | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/en/find-us/` | Address, directions, call, WhatsApp, hours |
| Wine list | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/vini/` | `https://www.osteriadeirossi.it/en/wine-list/` | The wine list, without prices |

A link that ends with `#something` opens the page at that place and skips the opening animation.
The table QR codes can keep pointing to `menu.osteriadeirossi.it` or move to `/menu/`: the founder decides.

## Share previews

Each page type shows its own picture when a link is shared: home (the osteria from outside), menu
(cinghiale in the coccio), wine list (the brick grotto), Dove siamo (the "Via dei Rossi" plate). A link
with an anchor shows the picture of its page. To check a preview, send the link to yourself on WhatsApp.

## Keeping it right

When a dish leaves the menu its address disappears with it: check the links of old posts when the menu
changes. DigiAlto checks once a month that every address in this file opens and lands in the right place.
