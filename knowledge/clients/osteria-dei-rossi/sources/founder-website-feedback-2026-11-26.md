## Decisions stated by the founder

- Version 2 is kept as it is. Version 3 is a new version beside it.
- Food prices appear only on the menu page. The home page shows no price.
- The home page shows no dish row (dish name, dotted line, price, menu description). The
  explanation of a dish stays; a dish is named inside the explanation where it is worth it.
- The animations and motion graphics of version 2 are fine and stay.

## Instructions for version 3

- "La casa": the three photos are too small. The photo changes while the visitor scrolls and the
  page stays in place; the change is fast, with a quick transition.
- More creative changes: more line icons, motion graphics, animations and transitions.
- Before changing anything, the creative, copywriting and frontend specialists look at version 2
  and say how version 3 can be better for marketing, SEO and appeal.
- The arrow in the buttons shows a dot (the small loop near its tail): remove it.
