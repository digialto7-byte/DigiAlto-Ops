## Instructions for version 2

- Order of the home page: the story first; then the dishes, typical and medieval; then pici,
  explained, with the new photos; then the other foods, with explanations such as what Cinta
  Senese is; a section for cantucci e Vin Santo.
- Keep the opening (the gate), the transitions and the photos.
- Pici section: a minimal fine-line animation of pici coming down the page.
- Cinghiale section: a minimal fine-line wild boar that comes into the picture.
- "Where we are" moves to its own page, with the address, how to reach the osteria and the map
  with its motion.
- Menu page: opens like an old book with the name "Osteria dei Rossi" and the Torre del Mangia
  as an animated skyline behind it, then takes the visitor quickly to the menu.
- Wine list page: a fine-line animation of a hand holding a bottle that pours a glass of wine,
  then the carta dei vini opens.
- Use the copywriter more: describe the foods, the traditions of Tuscany and how the osteria
  brings them to its guests.

## Answers given by the founder in chat on 2026-11-25 (question round)

- Approved for the website: pici are thick, hand-rolled strands of pasta made from flour and
  water, typical of the Siena area; at the osteria they come with white Cinta Senese ragù, duck
  ragù, cacio e pepe and wild boar ragù.
- Approved: Cinta Senese is the native pig breed of the Siena hills, dark with a white band (the
  "cinta") around its shoulders; it is painted in Ambrogio Lorenzetti's fresco of 1338–39 in
  Siena's Palazzo Pubblico and has protected-origin (DOP) status.
- Approved: cantucci are Tuscan almond biscuits; the tradition is to dip them in Vin Santo, the
  sweet Tuscan dessert wine, to end the meal.
- Approved: Chianti Classico (the Gallo Nero, the black rooster), Vino Nobile di Montepulciano
  and Brunello di Montalcino are the great reds of the Siena area; the wine list carries all
  three, plus Bolgheri, other Tuscan reds, whites and sparkling wines.
- Wine list on the website: publish the wines and producers as transcribed, **without prices**.
  The founder proofreads the names.
- The menu's opening line "Mettetevi comodi: qui si mangia come a casa, con la ciccia buona e il
  vino giusto." may be used as the welcome line of the story section.
