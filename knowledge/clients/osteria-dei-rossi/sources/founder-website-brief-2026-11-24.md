## Facts stated by the founder in this message

- The osteria is in the heart of Siena, inside the city walls.
- Its dishes come from the Middle Ages; the place has a rich history.
- Dishes named: pici, cinghiale in umido, fresh Tuscan truffles, fiorentina.
- A wide choice of wines, such as Chianti, Rosso di Montepulciano and Montalcino.
- Instagram profile: https://www.instagram.com/osteriadeirossi/
- There is no brand identity yet; DigiAlto is to write the story and shape the identity.

## Design and build instructions from this message

- The opening is one of Siena's gates (a "porta"), drawn as an animated graphic that opens and
  brings the home page up.
- Style: minimal, fine line. Storytelling that scrolls down smoothly and fast enough.
- Every part is shown with the supplied photos: some wide as backgrounds, some as tiles beside
  the text.
- The handover is a guide, not a rule; `02_SKILLS.md` is to be followed strictly.
- Competitors and the reference site are inspiration only, never to be copied.
- SEO: study competitors' sites, rankings, keywords and meta tags; aim for the top of Siena's
  restaurant and osteria searches.
- Use the agent hierarchy; QA before every answer.
