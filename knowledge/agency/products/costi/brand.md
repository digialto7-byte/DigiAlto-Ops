# Costì — product brand (approved by the founder 2026-11-13)

> Costì is DigiAlto's restaurant-management SaaS product. This is the **product** brand. Inside each venue, the venue's own
> brand applies (for Osteria Dei Rossi: `knowledge/clients/osteria-dei-rossi/profile.md`). DigiAlto's agency brand is
> `knowledge/agency/brand.md`. Source of everything below: `sources/brand-board-2026-11-13.webp`, approved "as shown" by the
> founder on 2026-11-13.

## Name and positioning
- Name: **Costì** (with the grave accent on the final ì). Trademark availability: **not checked yet** (founder action).
- Tagline on the board (English): "The complete management platform for restaurants and bars".
- Headline on the board (English): "Manage your restaurant, from costs to service."
- First tenant: Osteria Dei Rossi.

## Logo
- Mark: a chef's hat containing three ascending bars (hospitality + cost analysis + growth).
- Wordmark: bold, rounded lettering; a coral accent above the final ì.
- Variants: horizontal (mark + wordmark) on light, reversed white on Forest, compact app icon (mark on Forest rounded square),
  mark-only small icon.
- Vector files: **to be recreated as SVG from the board** (founder 2026-11-13); the SVGs replace the board once approved.

## Colour palette
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | #0E4D3A | Primary: brand, headers, primary surfaces |
| Coral | #FF7A5C | Accent: highlights, the ì mark, icons, important actions (see contrast rules) |
| Sage | #DCEDE6 | Support: light surfaces, chips, pattern |
| Off-white | #F7F8F6 | Background |
| Charcoal | #1F2933 | Text |

### Contrast rules (WCAG 2.2 AA, computed 2026-11-13)
- Charcoal on Off-white 13.85 · Forest on Off-white 9.21 · White on Forest 9.81 · Forest on Sage 8.08 · Charcoal on Sage 12.15 — all pass for text.
- **Coral on Off-white 2.41 and White on Coral 2.56 fail** for text. Coral is for accents, icons and large graphics only; a coral
  button carries **Charcoal text** (5.75, passes). Coral on Forest 3.82: large text / non-text graphics only (≥ 3:1).

## Typography
- Montserrat — headings, interface, emphasis.
- Inter — body text, tables, data, UI.
- Both open-licence (SIL OFL); self-hosted in the app (no Google Fonts requests).

## Brand elements
- Pattern of outlined chef's hats in Sage with a Coral stroke accent.
- Line icons for venue types: cloche, cocktail glass, cutlery, wine bottle and glass, burger, coffee cup.
