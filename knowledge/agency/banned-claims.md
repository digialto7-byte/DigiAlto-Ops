# Banned claims and practices

The reviewer fails any deliverable that contains these. They apply to DigiAlto's own material
and to everything produced for clients.

## Never claim
- Guaranteed results: "guaranteed #1 on Google", "guaranteed leads/sales/ROI", "double your
  revenue", "go viral".
- Unverifiable superlatives: "the best agency in Milan", "the leading", "number one", "award-
  winning" (unless a specific award is an approved fact).
- Statistics, percentages, client counts, years of experience or results not in `knowledge/`.
- Partnerships, certifications or tool partner status (e.g. "Microsoft Partner",
  "Google Partner") unless listed as an approved fact.
- Client names, logos or case studies without the client's approval recorded in their profile.

## Never produce
- Fake or incentivised reviews, testimonials or ratings. Review campaigns ask real customers
  only and never script the review.
- `aggregateRating` / `review` schema without real, approved review data.
- Content impersonating another business, person or brand.
- Copies of other agencies' or brands' copy, logos, characters or designs.
- Dark patterns: fake countdowns, fake scarcity, pre-ticked consent boxes, hidden costs.
- Tracking or cookies without a consent mechanism where the law requires one.

## Language to avoid
- "Cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "synergy", "unlock", "supercharge",
  "in today's fast-paced world".
- "Digialto" or "digialto" spelling.
