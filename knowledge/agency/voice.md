# DigiAlto voice

**Tone:** modern, confident, digital-first, forward-thinking.
**Voice:** clear, engaging, empowering, with a professional tech touch.

## Rules
- Lead with the outcome for the reader.
- Short, confident sentences. Explain tech in plain language.
- No hype without proof. Every claim must be supportable from `knowledge/`.
- Concrete over abstract: name the deliverable, the tool, the timeframe.
- No filler ("in today's fast-paced digital world", "unlock your potential", "game-changer").

## Channel rules
- **LinkedIn (DigiAlto and founder posts):** no em dashes or double dashes, no emojis.
- **Email:** subject line under 50 characters, one clear ask.
- **Website:** headline states the benefit, subheadline states how, CTA is a verb.

## Languages
DigiAlto works in English and Italian. Write each natively; do not translate literally.
